# Contributing

Pull requests are welcome. For anything bigger than a fix, open an issue first so we can agree on the approach before you spend time on it.

Before you send one, run `scripts/test.sh`, and for changes to Clicking, Scrolling or Moving, the live self-test in `scripts/selftest.sh`. [docs/ARCHITECTURE.md](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md) explains how the pieces fit together.

## Your rights in what you send

KeyNav's source is free to build for yourself, but the ready-made app is sold at [keynav.app](https://keynav.app). By opening a pull request, you confirm that the change is your own work, and you give Kevin Young a permanent, worldwide, royalty-free license to use, change and distribute it as part of KeyNav, including in the builds that are sold. You keep the copyright in your change.
